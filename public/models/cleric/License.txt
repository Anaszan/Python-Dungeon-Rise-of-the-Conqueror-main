Note on the .glb next to the .obj

The .glb in this folder is generated from the .obj by tools/rig_male.py: same
geometry, same textures, plus a fitted skeleton whose bone names match the
women pack in ../female so both genders load through one code path. In its bind
pose it renders identically to the .obj. Still CC0, still Quaternius' artwork.
